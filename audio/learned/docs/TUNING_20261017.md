## 调整依据与结果

### 第1次

配置：`configs/tuning_20261017/experiment01.yaml`
依据：Reduce previous Transformer overfitting; smaller LR, stronger head regularization/weight decay, mix clean and augmented samples; increase training budget for lower learning rate.
调整：`{"learning_rate": [0.001, 0.0005], "weight_decay": [0.0001, 0.001], "head_dropout": [0.2, 0.3], "augment_probability": [1, 0.5], "epochs": [30, 45], "patience": [7, 10]}`
结论：Transformer and CNN improved; BiLSTM regressed. Best validation accuracy rose from 47.08% to 52.92%; selected macro-F1 rose from 45.08% to 52.04%. No model or handoff file from the baseline was overwritten.

### 第2次

配置：`configs/tuning_20261017/experiment02.yaml`
依据：Round 1 CNN improved but BiLSTM regressed; neutral/weak classes motivated train-only inverse-frequency class weights, mild label smoothing and smaller-LR warm-start refinement. Parallel workers change execution only; numerical equivalence tested.
调整：`{"class_balance": true, "label_smoothing": 0.05, "learning_rate": 0.0002, "warm_start_round": 1, "epochs": 25, "patience": 8}`
结论：CNN and Transformer improved again; BiLSTM recovered from round 1 but remains slightly below the original baseline. Preserve baseline BiLSTM for the next warm start.

### 第3次

配置：`configs/tuning_20261017/experiment03.yaml`
依据：Transformer training/validation gap persists; lower LR to 1e-4 and increase augmented fraction to 75%. Roll BiLSTM back to original validation-best checkpoint rather than compound round 1 regression.
调整：`{"learning_rate": 0.0001, "augment_probability": 0.75, "epochs": 20, "patience": 6, "warm_start_best_of": true}`
结论：75% augmentation improved BiLSTM to macro-F1 .3914/accuracy .4375, but reduced CNN and Transformer. Global validation best remains round 2 Transformer.

### 第4次

配置：`configs/tuning_20261017/experiment04.yaml`
依据：Revert augmentation to 50% for the stronger CNN/Transformer branches; increase label smoothing and weight decay to address high train confidence and persistent validation gap while retaining best BiLSTM weights.
调整：`{"augment_probability": 0.5, "label_smoothing": 0.1, "weight_decay": 0.003, "warm_start_best_of": true}`
结论：Stronger weight decay and smoothing reduced validation loss, but all three primary macro-F1 results stayed below their historical best. Reject this configuration as final.

### 第5次

配置：`configs/tuning_20261017/experiment05.yaml`
依据：Return to less aggressive regularization near round 2; local small-LR refinement. Temper inverse-frequency weights with square root to reduce overcorrection of the minority neutral class. Keep all prior best checkpoints.
调整：`{"learning_rate": 5e-05, "head_dropout": 0.25, "label_smoothing": 0.05, "weight_decay": 0.001, "class_balance_power": 0.5, "epochs": 15, "patience": 5, "scheduler_patience": 2, "warm_start_best_of": true}`
结论：Final Transformer improves macro-F1 slightly over round 2, matching its highest selected-checkpoint accuracy. CNN and BiLSTM remain best in rounds 2 and 3 respectively.
